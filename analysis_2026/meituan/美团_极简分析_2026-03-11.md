## 2. Analysis Approach (Simplest Methods)

### 2.1 Value Investing / Fundamental Analysis

- **Business model**: Food delivery + in-store dining + travel + instant retail (Meituan Shangou) + overseas (KeeTa). Three-sided network effects, ~70% food delivery share, ~770M annual transacting users, 14.5M active merchants. [12]
- **Cash flow & DCF**: FY 2024 FCF RMB 34.7B, margin 13.67% [10][11]. Q3 2025 OCF turned negative (-RMB 22.1B) due to price war [12]. DCF-based intrinsic value HK$331.79 (AlphaSpread) vs current ~HK$77 implies large discount, but market doubts ability to restore cash flow amid competition [12].
- **Profitability拐点**: Q3 2025 first quarterly loss since late 2022; management expects losses into Q4; analysts expect unit economics to turn positive in Q1–Q2 2026 as subsidies taper [1][2]. **Basis**: Reuters [1], Morningstar [2], AlphaSpread [10].

### 2.2 Business Model & Corporate Culture

- Management opposes unsustainable price war; emphasizes service quality and high-order-value segments (15–30 RMB orders) [1][2]. Jan 2026 regulatory probe may support more rational competition [13][14].
- **Basis**: Reuters [1], Bloomberg [13].

### 2.3 Technical Analysis (Simplest)

- No complex models. Reference: consensus target HK$124.30 vs current ~HK$77; stock down >30% in 2025 [2]. Relative to target, current price implies ~38% discount.
- **Basis**: MarketScreener [21], Reuters [1].
